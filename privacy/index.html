Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

TinyToolbox is designed to be simple and privacy-conscious. This policy describes the current website and tools.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, along with the information normally processed by your email provider. We use it to respond to your request and maintain correspondence.

Browser-based tools

The JSON Formatter & Validator processes the text you enter locally in your browser. The tool does not intentionally transmit your JSON input to our server. Avoid entering confidential data into any online service unless you have assessed that service for your needs.

Server logs and essential operations

Our hosting provider may process standard technical information in connection with delivering and securing the website, such as request metadata, IP addresses, browser information, and timestamps, subject to the provider's systems and policies.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

At the time this policy was written, this site does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics or third-party advertising services are added in the future, this policy should be updated to describe those services and their data practices before or when they are enabled. Third-party providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Third-party links

This website may link to external websites. We do not control their privacy practices; review their policies when visiting them.

Children

This website is a general-purpose utility site and is not intended to solicit personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website evolves. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Contact
